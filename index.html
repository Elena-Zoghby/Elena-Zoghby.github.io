Early-exit inference for RepViT

Capstone of the LebNet AI Fellowship

I reproduced RepViT (CVPR 2024) and added an early-exit mechanism. When the penultimate stage is confident enough, judged by its softmax output, the network skips the final stage. It skipped that stage on about 17% of inputs with almost no accuracy loss.

Input→Penultimate stage→Confident enough?→Yes: output nowNo: run final stage

Hand gesture recognition on an ESP32-CAM

University project, supervised by Prof. Wassim Falou and Prof. Mayssa Hajar

An end-to-end TinyML system: a custom CNN, quantized to INT8 and deployed with TensorFlow Lite Micro. It recognizes six gestures plus a “no gesture” class and plays matching audio through a Python app. I captured 7,000+ images over three phases and kept a curated set of 2,000. Models that scored 94–96% in validation did worse on the actual device, so I shipped the one that performed best in real tests.

ESP32-CAM→INT8 CNN on device→Serial→Python app→Audio
